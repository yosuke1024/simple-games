## Summary

Simple Games apps (including **Number Match Offline**) are designed to work
fully offline.

- We do not require an account. There is no sign-up or login.
- We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or location.
- Your game progress, settings, and statistics are stored **only on your
  device**. We have no server that stores your data, and no cloud sync.
- There are no in-app purchases.
- Deleting the app (or using "Reset Local Data" in Settings) removes your
  game data.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only: current game
state, seeds, statistics, daily challenge progress, settings (language, theme,
sound, vibration, reduced motion), and ad frequency counters. This data never
leaves your device through any system we operate.

## Advertising (online only)

When your device is online, the app may show ads served by Google AdMob.
Google may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) to serve
and measure ads, as described in
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
Where required by law, the app shows a consent form before serving
personalized ads. When your device is offline, no ads are shown and no ad
requests are made. The game is fully playable without ads.

## Analytics

The app may use Firebase Analytics (free tier) to record a minimal set of
anonymous gameplay events (for example, "game started", "game completed") to
confirm the game works. These events contain no personal information, no
email address, and no board contents. Analytics never blocks gameplay and does
not run while offline. <!-- Firebase Analytics を配線しない場合はこの節を削除 -->

## Permissions

The app requests network access only, used exclusively for ads, ad consent,
remote configuration, and analytics. The app does not request location,
camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, phone, SMS, calendar, or
notification permissions.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children.
