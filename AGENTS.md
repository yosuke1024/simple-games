# Simple Games — agent entry point

Before planning, implementing, or reviewing changes in this repository, read:

1. `docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` — the common project source-of-truth entry point.
2. The canonical document(s) linked from it for the area you are changing.

Do not treat `docs/plans/`, old issues, or comments as current product policy when they conflict with canonical documents.
Do not infer product intent from common game-industry patterns: Simple Games intentionally rejects many of them.

For game changes, always read that game's `docs/*_RULES.md` before editing its implementation.
For storage/migration, monetization, web-only behavior, public copy, or i18n changes, follow the required-reading map in `docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`.
